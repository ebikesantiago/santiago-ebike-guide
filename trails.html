Trails & routes

Paved stars, soft-surface side quests, and honest notes about what works well with an e-bike.

From Santiago Township you’re a short drive from the best paved multi-use option in the area — plus wildlife and parkland that reward a slower pace (sometimes on foot).

Paved · Priority ride

Great Northern Regional Trail (GNRT)

The local crown jewel for e-bikes: an ~8.5-mile paved multi-use trail linking Elk River and Zimmerman (Lion’s / Zimmerman City Park area). It’s rails-to-trails on the old Great Northern Railroad corridor (operated ~1886–1976) — gentle grades, birding ponds, woods, and neighborhood crossings.

  • Surface: Paved — excellent for e-bikes, walking, inline skating
  • Ends: Elk Lake Rd NW & Upland St NW area (Elk River) ↔ Zimmerman City Park / Lion’s Park (~25850 Main St, Zimmerman)
  • Mid-trail access: Woodland Trails Regional Park (20135 Elk Lake Rd NW, Elk River) and other designated lots
  • County facility note: Trail info associated with Elk Lake Road NW, Elk River area
  • Parking: Multiple designated trailheads — check the current Sherburne County map before you go
  • Vision: ~30 miles Elk River → Milaca; part of broader loop ideas with Ports to Plains / Mississippi River Regional Trail (~100+ mile regional loop concepts)
  • Speed: County trail limit 20 mph (see Rules)
Construction / detours can pop up (e.g., Highway 169 & CSAH 4 work near Zimmerman has caused periodic closures). Always peek at the Sherburne County GNRT page for current status.

Sherburne National Wildlife Refuge

Accessible from the County Road 1 area near Santiago — a standout for wildlife viewing (waterfowl, prairie, oak woodland). Trails are largely natural-surface hiking.

  • Fantastic as a side trip from Santiago
  • E-bike rules may differ on refuge roads/trails — check USFWS before riding
  • Often best: park, then walk/hike for viewing

Mostly natural / crushed limestone

Oak Savanna Park (Becker)

10775 27th Ave SE, Becker — hiking, equestrian, and ski-oriented parkland with a crushed-limestone accessible loop near the Sherburne History Center / Clitty Lake overlook.

  • Connects conceptually toward Becker City Park
  • Honest take: pavement is limited — better as hike/walk, or verify surface before e-biking
  • Great cultural stop at Sherburne History Center nearby
Park & woods

Becker City Park

Wooded parkland with local connections toward the Oak Savanna area. Treat it as a soft-surface / park explore — not a substitute for the paved GNRT if you want a long e-bike spin.

Restrictions apply

Bibles State Wildlife Management Area

Nearby WMA land with restrictions typical of Minnesota WMAs (hunting seasons, vehicle limits, etc.). Not a casual e-bike destination — research MN DNR rules and seasons before any visit.

Trail-adjacent park

Woodland Trails Regional Park

Along the GNRT corridor in Elk River (~20135 Elk Lake Rd NW): woods, prairie, wetlands, picnic spots, and paved connectors to the Great Northern. Handy mid-ride stretch break.

Northern trail end

Zimmerman City Park / Lion’s Park

Northern paved terminus vibe — disc golf / frisbee course area, parking, and a natural turnaround for out-and-backs. Pair with a Zimmerman coffee stop when you’re done spinning.

Map context

Surface honesty policy: If it’s crushed limestone, dirt, or refuge trail, assume “verify first.” Your e-bike loves pavement; nature loves you walking quietly.
