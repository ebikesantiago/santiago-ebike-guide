Seasonal tip

Fall is prime time: cooler temps, fewer skeeters, and oak colors along the Great Northern. Pack a light layer, watch for leaf-slick pavement, and enjoy the prairie light. Spring? Expect mud on soft surfaces. Summer? Bug spray is not optional. Winter fat/e-bike only where legal, clear, and safe.

Where you are

Santiago Township, Sherburne County — flat-to-gently-rolling prairie, oak, and wetland country. The map below is centered near Santiago (~45.53, −93.82).

View larger map on OpenStreetMap

Honest trail talk: The Great Northern Regional Trail is the paved e-bike-friendly highlight (~8.5 miles Elk River ↔ Zimmerman). Places like Oak Savanna Park and Sherburne National Wildlife Refuge are fantastic — but surfaces and rules differ. Always verify current maps and policies with Sherburne County and USFWS.
