E-bike rules & etiquette

Sherburne County made the rules readable: electric-assisted bikes welcome — speed capped — play nice.

As e-bikes surged, Sherburne County updated its Parks Regulations Ordinance. Rather than trying to police Class 1 vs 2 vs 3 by eyeball (nearly impossible), they set a clear 20 mph speed limit on county trails — including the Great Northern Regional Trail.

What’s allowed on Sherburne County trails
Thing On county trails? Notes
Electric-assisted bicycles (state-law definition) Allowed Keep speed ≤ 20 mph
Electric personal assistive mobility devices (state-law definition) Allowed Same speed expectation
Traditional bikes, walking, inline skating Allowed Multi-use — share kindly
ATVs, golf carts, motorized vehicles Not allowed Ordinance treats these as vehicles barred from trails
Motorized bikes, scooters, hoverboards, etc. (as defined in ordinance) Not allowed Don’t invent loopholes — check county text if unsure
Why 20 mph? County staff noted e-bike classes are hard to tell apart visually, so a universal speed limit is more enforceable — and 20 mph is plenty fast among walkers, dogs, and kids.

Trail etiquette (the unwritten rules that keep everyone happy)

📣

Announce when passing

“On your left!” — early, friendly, not a foghorn. Slow a touch as you go by.

🐕

Yield & soft pedal

Yield to situations that need space: dogs on leashes, strollers, folks stopping for photos of a heron.

🐢

Control speed near people

Assist mode is a gift — not a dare. Near clusters of walkers, dial it down.

💡

Lights at dusk

Prairie evenings sneak up. Front white + rear red make you a good neighbor.

Other lands = other rulebooks

  • Sherburne National Wildlife Refuge: USFWS rules govern roads/trails — e-bike access may be limited. Verify before you ride; hiking is often the better wildlife move.
  • Oak Savanna / city parks / WMAs: Surfaces and vehicle rules vary. When in doubt, walk or call the managing agency.
  • Roads between trailheads: Normal Minnesota traffic laws apply — mirrors, signals, visibility.
