Stops & spots

Trailheads, parks, wildlife, history — plus a few cafés we could actually verify. Hours change; peek before you roll.

Trailheads & parks

🅿️

GNRT — Elk River end

Southern paved corridor near Elk Lake Road NW & Upland Street NW. County facility references include the Elk Lake Road NW area. Multiple designated lots exist — use the official county parking map.

🥏

Lion’s / Zimmerman City Park

Northern vibe check: ~25850 Main St, Zimmerman — parking by the disc golf / frisbee course area. Classic out-and-back turnaround.

🌲

Woodland Trails Regional Park

20135 Elk Lake Rd NW, Elk River — mid-route park access, picnic, woods/prairie/wetlands, paved connectors to the Great Northern.

🏛️

Sherburne History Center

Near Oak Savanna Park in Becker — culture break + Clitty Lake overlook vicinity. Pair with a walk on crushed limestone loops.

🌳

Oak Savanna Park

10775 27th Ave SE, Becker — hiking/equestrian/ski character. Soft surfaces dominate; e-bike only if you’ve checked conditions.

🦆

Sherburne National Wildlife Refuge

Near Santiago via County Road 1 area access. Wildlife first, wheels second — confirm USFWS e-bike/road rules. Bring binoculars energy.
